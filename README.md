# Metagenomic analysis workflow for:

> Malas J, Zhao L, Landeche M, Sidebottom AM, Little J, Hampton-Marcell J, Sargis RM.
> *Home is Where the Heterogeneity Is: Housing Facility-Level Differences in the Gut Microbiome
> and Metabolic Phenotype Confound Arsenic Effects on Glucose Homeostasis in Male Mice.*
> BioRxiv pre-print: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.03.742222v1

## What this study did

Two parallel inorganic arsenic (50 mg/L sodium arsenite) drinking-water exposures were
run in male C57BL/6J mice at two animal facilities in the same city, in a 2 × 2 design
crossing facility with treatment. Metabolic phenotyping, hepatic trace metals, targeted
and untargeted cecal metabolomics, and shotgun metagenomics were compared across the
four groups. Housing facility explained more variance than arsenic treatment in every
data type, and the direction of the arsenic effect on glucose clearance differed between
the two sites.

## Data availability

Raw data are deposited publicly.

- **Shotgun metagenomes** — NCBI Sequence Read Archive, BioProject [`PRJNA1512446`](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1512446)
- **Host reference** — *Mus musculus* GRCm39 primary assembly, Ensembl release 115
